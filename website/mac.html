Getting it open

Apple charges for a signature the same way Microsoft does, so macOS treats Casrion as a stranger exactly once. Here is the whole dance.

  1. Drag it into Applications

    Straight out of the .dmg, like anything else.

  2. Right click it, choose Open

    Then Open again in the dialog. Double clicking will not work the first time, and that is deliberate on Apple's part. Still refusing? Run xattr -cr /Applications/Casrion.app in Terminal once, then open it normally.

  3. Turn on Accessibility

    System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. This is what lets Casrion read the text you highlighted in another app. Without it, explain comes up empty. Casrion opens that pane for you the first time you ask it to explain something.

  4. Turn on Input Monitoring, if you want it

    Same place, one row down. macOS guards raw mouse taps separately from the switch above, which catches people out. All this one does now is let the explain popup notice you clicking away from it. Skip it and everything still works. The popup closes when you switch to another app, or with its own close button.

  5. Quit and reopen, properly

    From the menu bar icon, not by closing the window. macOS only applies those switches to a fresh launch. The microphone and browser questions show up on their own when you first need them; click Allow.

The explain thing

Highlight anything, anywhere, press CmdShiftE, and a small window tells you what it says. It reads the screen around your selection first, so it knows a loose symbol belongs to the formula three lines up.

The first time you use it, it downloads a small model, once. After that it works on a plane. Nothing you highlight is ever sent anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to be sent to.

When something acts up

It says Casrion cannot be opened.
Right click it in Applications and choose Open, then Open again. Double clicking will not do it. If it still refuses, xattr -cr /Applications/Casrion.app in Terminal once.
Explain says nothing is selected, but I selected something.
Accessibility is off, or it is on for an older copy of the app. Turn it on, then quit Casrion from the menu bar and reopen it. A few apps also genuinely keep their selection to themselves, and Casrion will say so when that is what happened.
The explain popup will not go away when I click somewhere.
Same switch. Noticing a click outside its own window is exactly the thing Input Monitoring governs. Turn it on, then quit and reopen. Casrion spots this happening and offers you the settings pane from inside the popup.
I updated and a permission stopped working.
macOS ties permissions to the exact copy of the app, so the checkmarks can go stale while still looking switched on. Select Casrion in Accessibility and Input Monitoring, remove it with −, add it back with +, then quit and reopen.
Where do my notes live?
Markdown files in folders you picked, with screenshots, voice memos and whiteboards in an assets folder beside them. Nothing is in a database. They are just files.
